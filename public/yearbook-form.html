What is a professional headshot?

A simple shoulders-up photo where your face is easy to see. Think LinkedIn or a class directory, not a party picture or a full-body shot.

  • Face the camera and keep a natural expression.
  • Use steady, front-facing light — avoid shadows or harsh glare.
  • Use a plain, uncluttered background.
  • Keep glasses clean and avoid heavy filters.
  • Crop so your head and shoulders fill the frame.
